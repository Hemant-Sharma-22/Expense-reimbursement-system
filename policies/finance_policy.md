# Corporate Finance Policy

## Section 1: Reimbursement Submission Timelines
- Expense reimbursement requests must be submitted within **30 days** of incurring the expense or completing business travel.
- Requests submitted after 60 days will require executive CFO approval and may be subject to taxable income treatment.

## Section 2: Approval Hierarchy & Escalation Levels
- Expenses under **$1,000 USD**: Approved by direct Department Manager.
- Expenses between **$1,000 USD and $5,000 USD**: Approved by Department Manager and Finance Director.
- Expenses exceeding **$5,000 USD**: Require VP and CFO approval.

## Section 3: Reimbursement Payout SLAs
- Approved reimbursement requests will be processed and disbursed within **5 business days** of final manager approval via direct deposit.

## Section 4: Audit & Compliance Checks
- The Finance Audit team performs random sample audits on 15% of all submitted reimbursements monthly.
- Any intentional falsification of receipts or duplicate reimbursement claims will result in immediate disciplinary action up to termination.
