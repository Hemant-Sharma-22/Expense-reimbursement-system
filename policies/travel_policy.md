# Corporate Travel Policy

## Section 1: Travel Authorizations and Booking
- All domestic and international business travel must be approved in writing by a Department Manager at least **14 days** prior to departure.
- Travel bookings should be made using preferred corporate travel channels or discount providers whenever possible.

## Section 2: Air Travel
- Employees are required to book **Economy Class** for all domestic flights and international flights under 6 hours.
- **Premium Economy or Business Class** is permitted only for non-stop international flights exceeding **8 hours**, subject to VP approval.
- Seat selection fees exceeding $50.00 USD and flight upgrade fees are not reimbursable.

## Section 3: Lodging and Accommodations
- Hotel room rates should not exceed **$250.00 USD per night** in standard cities or **$400.00 USD per night** in high-cost tier 1 cities (e.g., New York, London, Tokyo, San Francisco).
- Personal room charges such as minibar items, laundry (unless trip exceeds 5 consecutive days), and spa services are non-reimbursable.

## Section 4: Ground Transportation & Car Rentals
- Public transit, rideshare (UberX/Lyft), and airport shuttles are preferred mode of ground transport.
- Compact or Mid-size car rentals are allowed when public transit is impractical.
- Rental car insurance (Collision Damage Waiver) should be declined if covered under corporate umbrella policy.
