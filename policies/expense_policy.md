# Corporate Expense Policy

## Section 1: Overview and Purpose
This document sets forth the corporate rules and guidelines governing business expenses incurred by employees on behalf of the company. All expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and incurred for legitimate business purposes.

## Section 2: General Receipt Requirements
- Itemized receipts are mandatory for any expense exceeding **$25.00 USD**.
- Digital copies, clear photographs, or PDF receipts uploaded via the Expense Reimbursement System are acceptable.
- Credit card slips without itemized details are not sufficient for expenses over $50.00 USD.
- Receipts must clearly display the merchant name, transaction date, items purchased, and total amount paid.

## Section 3: Expense Categories and Threshold Limits

### 3.1 Meals & Entertainment
- Individual Meal Allowance: Up to **$75.00 USD** per day.
- Team Lunch / Client Entertainment: Up to **$300.00 USD** per event, requiring prior Manager approval.
- Alcoholic beverages will not be reimbursed unless consumed during an authorized client dinner.

### 3.2 Office Supplies & Accessories
- Standard Workstation Equipment (monitors, keyboards, mice, cables): Up to **$500.00 USD** per calendar year.
- Ergonomic Chairs or Desk Accessories: Up to **$350.00 USD**, requiring HR or Manager approval.

### 3.3 Software & Cloud Subscriptions
- Developer SaaS subscriptions, API tools, and software licenses: Up to **$1,000.00 USD** per year.
- Recurring monthly subscriptions must be submitted with an annual invoice or monthly receipt.

## Section 4: Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following items are strictly non-reimbursable:
- Personal entertainment, movies, or gaming subscriptions.
- Fines, parking tickets, or traffic violations.
- Commuting costs from home to the primary office location.
- Lost or damaged personal property.
