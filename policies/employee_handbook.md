# Employee Handbook

## Section 1: Work Hours & Remote Work Policies
- Standard company core working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time.
- Remote work stipend: Full-time remote employees receive a one-time setup allowance of **$500 USD** for home office setup, plus a monthly internet allowance of **$50 USD**.

## Section 2: Continuing Education & Professional Development
- Employees with over 6 months of tenure are eligible for up to **$1,500 USD per year** for professional courses, certifications, and technical conferences.
- Prior written approval from Department Manager is required before enrolling.

## Section 3: Health & Wellness Benefit
- Monthly wellness reimbursement of up to **$50 USD** is available for gym memberships, fitness equipment, or mental health apps.

## Section 4: Code of Conduct & Integrity
- Employees must maintain transparency and honesty in all financial disclosures and corporate transactions.
- Reporting compliance violations can be done confidentially through the Ethics Hotline.
